Report a lost item

Tell us what is missing, when you last had it and where. The security office compares your report with every item handed in, and emails you as soon as one matches. Reports are sent from your student or staff account, so create an account first if you do not have one.

When and where

Before you report

  • Check the found items list. It may already have been handed in.
  • Lost a phone? Call it from a friend's phone first, and lock it from your account on another device.
  • Lost your ID card? Report it to the registry as well, so a replacement can be issued.

Notices

  • Found items are kept for 90 days, then donated, recycled or returned to whoever issued them.
  • Bring your student or staff ID card whenever you collect an item.
  • Found cash? Hand it straight to the security office.
