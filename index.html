Lost something on campus? Found something that is not yours?

The Solution Lost and Found is where students and staff report what they have lost and what they have found, in one place. The campus security office checks every report, matches lost items with found ones, and hands an item back only to the person who can prove it is theirs.

Every report asks the same four things: what kind of item it is, what it looks like, when, and where. Asking the same four things every time is what lets a lost report and a found report meet.

What would you like to do?

  • Report a lost item

    Tell us what is missing, when you last had it and where.

  • Search found items

    See what has been handed in to the security office in the last two weeks.

  • Report a found item

    Picked something up? Tell us where you found it, then hand it in.

  • Create an account

    Students and staff need an account to send a report or a claim.

Who it is for

  • Students report lost and found items, search what has been handed in, and claim what is theirs.
  • Staff, teaching and non-teaching, do the same, and can log items left at their own office.
  • The security office records every found item, checks every claim and hands items back.

Notices

  • Found items are kept for 90 days, then donated, recycled or returned to whoever issued them.
  • Bring your student or staff ID card whenever you collect an item.
  • Found cash? Hand it straight to the security office.
