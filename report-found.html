Report a found item

Thank you for stopping. Tell us what you found and where, then hand it in to the security office on the ground floor of the Student Centre. The office keeps it safe, and gives it only to the person who proves it is theirs.

Do not type serial numbers, names on cards, or what is inside a bag. The officer records those privately when you hand the item in. They are how the real owner is recognised.
