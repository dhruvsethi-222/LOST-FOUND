Why we built it

Every week, phones, ID cards, keys, water bottles and laptop chargers are left behind in lecture halls, the library and the cafeteria. Most of them are picked up by someone honest. The trouble is that the person who lost the item and the person who found it never meet: notices on the board are missed, class group chats move too fast, and the box behind the security desk is a mystery to anyone who has not been told it exists.

The Solution Lost and Found puts both sides in one place, and puts the security office in charge of every item that goes back to its owner.

Who is behind it

The site works with the campus security office, which keeps every found item, checks every claim and signs every item out. It was built by The Solution, a team of two Diploma in Business Information Technology students, as the team project for DBT 1305 Web Application Development.

What the system will never allow

  • A report or a claim from anyone without a verified student or staff account.
  • Anyone but the security office seeing a found item's serial number, marks or contents.
  • An item leaving the office without an approved claim and the collector's ID card.
  • Two people being given the same item.
  • A report with a missing detail, or with a date that has not happened yet.
  • A decision or a hand-over being changed or deleted once it is recorded.
  • A found item being kept past the 90-day retention period.
  • One student or staff member seeing another's reports or claims.
  • Students or staff creating security office accounts.
