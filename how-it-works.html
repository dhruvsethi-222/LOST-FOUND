From lost to back in your hands

There are six steps between an item going missing and its owner taking it home. The security office is part of every one of them.

  1. 1 · Report

    The owner reports a lost item. Anyone who picks something up reports a found item and hands it in to the security office.

  2. 2 · Match

    The system compares lost and found reports by category, description, date and place, and puts the closest matches first for the security officer.

  3. 3 · Claim

    The owner finds the item on the found items list and sends a claim, answering questions only the owner could answer.

  4. 4 · Verify

    The security officer reads the claim beside the private details recorded when the item came in, then approves it, rejects it or asks for more evidence.

  5. 5 · Hand over

    The owner collects the item. The officer records the collector's ID card number, the date and their own name.

  6. 6 · Dispose

    An item nobody claims within 90 days is donated, recycled or returned to whoever issued it, and its status is saved as Disposed.

What to bring when you collect

Bring your student or staff ID card every time. The officer also asks for proof that depends on the kind of item.

Proof of ownership the security office asks for
Kind of item What you show or tell the officer Why
Phones and laptops Unlock it in front of the officer, or give the serial number or IMEI Anyone can describe a black phone; only the owner can open it
ID and bank cards A second photo ID that shows the same name The name on the card must be the person collecting it
Bags and wallets What is inside, before the officer opens it The contents are never shown on the site, so only the owner knows them
Keys The keyring and tags, and what each key opens Keys open rooms, lockers and homes
Clothing, books and everything else Size, brand, marks, or a name written inside A few exact details are enough for everyday things

How long items are kept

Found items are kept for 90 days. After that, clothing and everyday items go to charity, electronics are wiped and recycled, and ID and bank cards go back to the office or bank that issued them. Every disposal is recorded with the officer's name, the date and the reason.
