Contact the security office

The campus security office runs The Solution Lost and Found. For anything about a report, a claim or an item you want to collect, these are the people to reach.

Where to find us

Campus Security Office
Ground floor, Student Centre
Main campus, Nairobi

Telephone

  • 0720 000 000 — the security desk, during opening hours
  • 0733 000 000 — the main gate, at any hour

Email

Write to lostandfound@campus.example and we will answer within one working day. Put the item's reference in the subject, for example LF-0146.

Before you come to collect

  • Bring your student or staff ID card.
  • Bring the reference from the found items list.
  • Be ready to describe something only the owner would know.

Security office

Ground floor, Student Centre

  • Monday to Friday, 8.00am to 6.00pm
  • Saturday, 9.00am to 1.00pm
  • Sunday and public holidays: closed

Desk: 0720 000 000

Notices

  • Found items are kept for 90 days, then donated, recycled or returned to whoever issued them.
  • Bring your student or staff ID card whenever you collect an item.
  • Found cash? Hand it straight to the security office.
