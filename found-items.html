Found items

Everything handed in to the security office in the last two weeks. To protect owners, the list shows only the kind of item, where it was found and when. The description, serial numbers and anything inside stay with the security office, and they are what the officer asks you about when you claim.

Claim pending means an owner has sent a claim and the security officer is checking it. Returned items stay on the list for one week after they are collected, so the finder can see that the item reached its owner.

Seen something that could be yours? Note its reference and take it to the security office with your student or staff ID card.

Notices

  • Found items are kept for 90 days, then donated, recycled or returned to whoever issued them.
  • Bring your student or staff ID card whenever you collect an item.
  • Found cash? Hand it straight to the security office.
